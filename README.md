The process:
I did the LDR project during a sustainable engineering design lab. Firstly, I assembled the circuit on a breadboard to make sure it works properly. 
After that I placed the components on the copper board and soldered them. My first attempt was unsuccessful as I joined 2 rows while soldering and due to the wrong 
component placement. On my seccond attempt I placed the components on the right place and made sure I don't connect 2 rows while soldering.

How the circuit works:
When it is light, the resistance of the LDR (Light Dependant Resistor) is low causing the base voltage on transistor T1 to be low thus switching off the transistor,
therefore no current flows through the LED. At night when it is dark the resistance of the LDR is high, causing the base voltage to increase thus switching the 
transistor on. Current flows through the transistor and the LED, causing it to light up.
